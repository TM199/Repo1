But I thought: *this is just pattern matching*.

Today, that same research runs automatically:
- 11 data sources monitored 24/7
- 13 signal types detected
- Contacts enriched in real-time
- Delivered to CRM before coffee

The recruiter? He takes meetings now. Not research.

**The technical magic (simplified):**

The Vercel AI SDK lets you define "tools"—discrete capabilities an AI agent can use.

Tools we built:
- `search_company` → Find company info via web search
- `classify_agency` → Is this a recruitment agency? (filter them out)
- `fetch_companies_house` → Get UK registration data
- `enrich_contact` → Find decision-maker emails

The AI agent orchestrates these tools based on what it needs.

You give it a goal: "Find companies with hiring pain that match this ICP."

It figures out which tools to use and in what order.

**The insight that changed my thinking:**

Every recruiter I spoke to described their BD process as:
"I look for [specific signals] that indicate [buying intent]"

AI doesn't replace the formula. It executes it at infinite scale.

The human intelligence that identifies good signals is irreplaceable.

The manual execution of detecting those signals is not.
